Offer / Need wall — everything at a glance

Two columns side by side: what the group is offering on the left, what the group needs on the right. Categories that appear in BOTH columns light up green (offer side) or amber (need side) so the eye jumps to overlap. Search + explicit filter chips at the top; the filter join is spelled out ("filter offers AND needs by category: Housing"). Great for hosts scanning what the group has and needs at a glance.
