2What is publicly visible

Your profile page info on Appear Network is publicly accessible once completed and is discoverable through search. The following are visible to anyone who visits your page by direct link or QR code and to all network members, without signing in:

  • Your profile photo and your name
  • Your headline, the ways you work, and the services you offer
  • Your skills, interests, values, and the domains you work in
  • Your languages and the topics you invite people to ask you about
  • Anything you write in a free-text field on your profile, including what you are seeking or need help with

People who are not members and who are not signed in can view your page, and search engines may index it.

Approximate location. If you provide a postal code or city, it is used to suggest people, events, and opportunities near you, and it is readable alongside your public profile. We do not ask for your street address.

Contact information. Your contact info is only shared in the way you wish. If you mutually connect with someone. Supporting members can choose to share their contact info with anyone. If you put contact details into a free-text field on your profile, they are public in the same way as the rest of your profile.

Job matching. Within the job matching feature, we do not reveal your name, photo, or contact information to the other party until both of you agree to connect. This applies inside the matching feature only. It does not make your profile page private. Anyone holding your page link, or who finds your page through search, can see your photo and name at any time.

Event participation. When you register for an event you choose at that moment whether to appear in the attendee list and that choice is set to visible unless you change it. If you appear in the attendee list, the following are visible to others, including people who are not signed in:

  • Your first name and initials
  • A link to your profile page
  • Which events you registered for and when

If you host an event, your name is published with the event listing, along with the venue and the date and time.
